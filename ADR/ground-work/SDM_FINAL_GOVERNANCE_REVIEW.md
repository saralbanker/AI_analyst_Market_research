# SDM_FINAL_GOVERNANCE_REVIEW
**Framework:** Decision Governance Adjudication Framework (DGAF)
**Target:** SDM_V1.1 Governance Ambiguities

---

## 1. Portfolio Drawdown Governance (PDG)
### Step-1: Identify
* **Ambiguity:** Risk-C01 proved that a portfolio-level 5% hard halt cannot be executed without autonomous market orders, which strictly violates `ODP-001` (Human approval mandatory for all trade actions).
* **Cause:** Conflict between capital preservation limits and absolute non-autonomy.

### Step-2: Generate Options
* **Option A: The Liquidation Exemption.** Amend ODP-001 to grant the SDM strictly bounded authority to execute *Market Sell Orders ONLY*, exclusively during a 5% aggregate portfolio breach.
* **Option B: The Synthetic Portfolio Stop.** Maintain zero autonomy. Force the SDM to mathematically constrain individual entry stop-losses so tightly that an aggregate 5% drawdown is mathematically impossible, even if all positions gap down simultaneously.
* **Option C: Pure Notification.** Alert the human and accept the risk of catastrophic flash-crash ruin if the human is unavailable.

### Step-3: Evaluate
* **Option A** solves the risk but violates ODP-001.
* **Option B** preserves ODP-001 and guarantees risk limits, but causes severe opportunity starvation (stop-losses will be too tight for swing trading volatility).
* **Option C** violates fundamental Capital Preservation.

### Step-4: Recommend Winner
**Winner: Option A (The Liquidation Exemption).** Future SaaS compatibility and absolute capital preservation dictate that the system must have a catastrophic safety net independent of human latency.
**Owner Decision Required:** YES.

## 2. Human Override Governance (HOG)
### Step-1: Identify
* **Ambiguity:** If the human manually cancels a pre-authorized broker stop-loss (revenge trading/bypassing risk), does the SDM have authority to enforce governance against the Owner?
* **Cause:** Boundaries of SDM enforcement against human non-compliance are undefined.

### Step-2: Generate Options
* **Option A: Passive Logging.** The SDM records the override in the attribution log but allows the human to continue deploying new capital.
* **Option B: SDM Lockout.** The SDM detects the risk violation and algorithmically locks out the generation of new Signal Discoveries (SDM-04) and Capital Allocations (SDM-09) until the portfolio risk is brought back into compliance.

### Step-3: Evaluate
* **Option B** acts as a behavioral circuit breaker, preventing the human from compounding a revenge-trading scenario, aligning with the "Human System Analysis" findings of the Proof Audit.

### Step-4: Recommend Winner
**Winner: Option B (SDM Lockout).** The SDM must protect the capital from the operator's psychological drift.
**Owner Decision Required:** YES.

## 3. Opportunity Ranking Governance (ORG)
### Step-1: Identify
* **Ambiguity:** Does the human operator have to accept or reject trades in the exact hierarchical order presented by SDM-08 (Ranking)?
* **Cause:** Unclear ownership of final menu selection.

### Step-2: Generate Options
* **Option A: Strict Pipeline.** The human must act sequentially. Reject Rank 1 to see Rank 2.
* **Option B: Open Menu.** The human sees all EV-filtered, positively-ranked trades and may discretionary pick Rank #4 while rejecting Rank #1.

### Step-3: Evaluate
* **Option B** maximizes "Human Final Authority" (ODP-001) while remaining mathematically safe (since all trades on the menu have already passed the binary EV filter of Gap-03).

### Step-4: Recommend Winner
**Winner: Option B (Open Menu).** It provides human discretionary autonomy within a mathematically safe sandbox.
**Owner Decision Required:** NO.

## 4. Exit Hierarchy Governance (EHG)
### Step-1: Identify
* **Ambiguity:** Conflicting exit rationales. A trade hits its Time-Horizon limit (SDM-12), but the technical trend is accelerating.
* **Cause:** Exit precedence undefined.

### Step-2: Generate Options
* **Option A: Risk > Time > Technical.** Time forces exit regardless of trend.
* **Option B: Risk > Technical > Time.** Time horizon is extended if Technical momentum remains intact.

### Step-3: Evaluate
* **Option B** aligns with "Missing Winners > Over-Filtering". Cutting a winning trade purely based on calendar days limits upside capture.

### Step-4: Recommend Winner
**Winner: Option B (Risk > Technical > Time).**
**Owner Decision Required:** NO.

---

## 5. Attribution Scope Governance (ASG)
### Step-1: Identify
* **Ambiguity:** Should the system attribute trades where the human manually deviated from the SDM's recommended size or exit?
* **Cause:** Measuring "System Alpha" vs "Human Alpha".

### Step-2: Generate Options
* **Option A:** Only attribute pure system signals.
* **Option B:** Attribute System Signals (Baseline) AND track the delta generated by Human Overrides (Human Alpha/Bleed).

### Step-3: Evaluate
* **Option B** is essential to determine if the human operator is adding value to the SDM or destroying its mathematical edge.

### Step-4: Recommend Winner
**Winner: Option B (Delta Attribution).**
**Owner Decision Required:** NO.
